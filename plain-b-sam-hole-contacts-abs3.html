Robust Dipicolinic Acid-Anchored Self-Assembled Monolayers for High-Performance Wide-Bandgap and Tandem Perovskite Photovoltaics

2026-10-19 · Advanced Materials · DOI 10.1002/adma.75443 · [B1 F2*]

Yuxiao Guo, Yiren Gao, Daqing Zhang, Xu Li, Chong Zhang, Esmaeil Sheibani, Jinhai Huang, Tingwei He, Lars Kloo, Bo Xu

ABSTRACT The anchoring group of self-assembled monolayer (SAM) hole-transporting materials dictates both substrate binding and interfacial passivation, serving as a key lever for high-performance perovskite photovoltaics. Herein, we report a novel SAM molecular material, denoted by XS21 , engineered with a multidentate dipicolinic acid (DPA) anchoring core to address conventional adhesion and degradation limitations. From an experimental perspective, the DPA anchor is found to promote molecular chemisorption onto the substrate, potentially originating from the dual-action mechanism of pyridine-Ni coordination coupled with dicarboxylate-metal interaction. Furthermore, XS21 imparts both low acid-dissociation constants ( K a ) and superior ultraviolet (UV) photostability. Consequently, wide-bandgap (1.79 eV) perovskite solar cells containing XS21 deliver a champion power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 21.54% with an exceptional open-circuit voltage of 1.372 V. Crucially, integrating XS21 into monolithic all-perovskite tandem solar cells yields a remarkable PCE of 29.62%, alongside an outstanding operational stability (retaining >90% of initial efficiency after 900 h of continuous operation). This work establishes multi-dentate pyridine-carboxylate anchoring as a vital paradigm for next-generation efficient and stable perovskite photovoltaics.

Cooperative Dual-Directional π-π Stacking in Self-Assembled Monolayers for High-Performance Inverted Perovskite Solar Cells

Tao Guo, Yaning Li, Zongyuan Yang, Mengyuan Li, Zhe Sun, Jiakai Gu, Xueping Zong, Zhihui Wang, Yang Wang, Mao Liang

ABSTRACT The evolution from monomeric to dimeric self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) opens new opportunities for inverted perovskite solar cells (PSCs), yet stronger π - π interactions in these larger architectures risk inducing aggregation over ordered assembly, making it challenging to maintain a well-balanced intermolecular packing. We design a symmetric indenocarbazole dimer (DIC) to harness cooperative face-to-face and edge-to-edge π - π stacking for directing intermolecular attraction into ordered, long-range assembly rather than uncontrolled aggregation. In stark contrast, its asymmetric indenocarbazole-carbazole conjugate (ICCB) suffers from random clustering and incomplete passivation. This ordered SAM template provides a homogeneous foundation for perovskite crystallization, delivering a champion PCE of 26.33% with an outstanding FF of 86.62%, significantly outperforming ICCB (24.80%), while retaining over 85% of its initial efficiency after 1600 h. Our findings establish molecular symmetry and dual-directional π - π interactions as critical design principles for high-performance dimeric SAMs.

ABSTRACT High efficiency inverted perovskite solar cells often rely on nickel-containing hole-selective interfaces such as NiOx. However, the use of nickel presents sustainability challenges, as the material is inherently toxic and cross-contamination during end-of-life processing can compromise material recovery and recyclability. Here, we develop a nickel-free hole-selective interface material based on phenyl-substituted phosphonic-acid carbazole self-assembled monolayers (Ph-nPACz). Interfacial Multidentate Chelation Enabled Anti-Aggregation Self-Assembled Monolayers for High-Performance Inverted Perovskite Solar Cells

Xue Lu, Junlin Zhu, Kunpeng Li, Shuang Dong, Shichao Sun, Yunkun Liu, Linrong Li, Zifan Cui, Mei Dai, Xing Zhu, Hua Wang, Jiangzhao Chen, Tao Zhu

ABSTRACT The quality of self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) and the defect density at the perovskite buried interface are key factors determining the performance of inverted perovskite solar cells (IPSCs). However, the inherent aggregation of SAM molecules at this interface limits further efficiency improvements. Herein, we develop an interfacial multidentate chelation (IMC) strategy that integrates anti-aggregation functionality into SAMs for buried interface regulation. Specifically, 2,2′:6′,2′′-terpyridine (TERPY) is introduced into the SAM of [4-(3,6-dimethoxy-9H-carbazol-9-yl)butyl]phosphonic acid (MeO-4PACz). The π-π interactions between MeO-4PACz and TERPY suppress SAM self-aggregation, resulting in a compact and uniform SAM layer. Meanwhile, the three pyridinic nitrogen atoms in TERPY chelate with undercoordinated Pb 2 + defects through multidentate Pb-N coordination, reducing interfacial defect states. Benefiting from these dual effects, the champion power conversion efficiencies (PCEs) of small-area (0.043 cm 2 ) and large-area (1 cm 2 ) IPSCs reach 26.23% and 25.43%, respectively. Encapsulated devices retain 82.75% of their initial PCE after 1300 h of maximum power point tracking (MPPT) under the ISOS-L-2 protocol. This work provides a feasible strategy for simultaneously regulating SAM assembly and passivating buried interface defects.

Machine-Learning-Guided Design of Semirigid Hole-Selective Self-Assembled Monolayers for High-Efficiency and Stable Perovskite Solar Cells

Qi Zhang, Asmat Ullah, Jianyao Huang, Caner Değer, Ilhan Yavuz, Ruida Xu, Yiguo Yao, Vladyslav Hnapovskyi, Sofiia Kosar, Kai Wang, Stefaan De Wolf

Abstract Inverted perovskite solar cells (PSCs) require efficient, aggregation-resistant hole-transporting self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) at the buried interface. Conventional carbazole-based SAMs, such as 4PACz, suffer from excessive π-π stacking-driven aggregation, leading to inhomogeneous films, elevated trap-state densities, and accelerated device degradation. Here, we report a machine-learning (ML)-guided design strategy employing a pairwise-ranking Siamese directed message-passing neural network (D-MPNN) trained on a curated data set of published SAM control-target pairs. Statistical analysis of the data set reveals that the reduced tail-group planarity improves PCE relative to carbazole benchmarks. Guided by this insight, we designed two semirigid seven-membered-ring SAMs, 4-(5H-dibenzo[b,f]azepin-5-yl)butyl)phosphonic acid (4PABAP, ML score: +2.54) and 4-(10,11-dihydro-5H-dibenzo[b,f]azepin-5-yl)butyl)phosphonic acid (4PAHBAP, ML score: + 5.52) that the model ranked substantially above 4PACz (score: -0.32). Ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) simulations confirm that the nonplanar seven-membered rings suppress π-π stacking, enhance ITO-surface anchoring (interaction energies: 4PAHBAP -1.23 eV vs 4PACz -0.74 eV), and promote stronger charge transfer at the SAM/perovskite interface. Experimentally, 4PAHBAP-based inverted PSCs achieve a champion power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 26.36% with a fill factor (FF) of 84.35% significantly outperforming 4PACz-based controls (23.52%, FF 77.51%). Moreover, 4PAHBAP-based devices retain 92.5% of initial PCE after 1,200 h of continuous 1-sun maximum-power-point (MPP) operation under ISOS-L-3 standards, and 95.7% after 1,200 h thermal aging at 85 °C (ISOS-D-2). This work establishes a generalizable ML-first-then-validate paradigm for rational SAM design that simultaneously maximizes efficiency and long-term operational durability.
