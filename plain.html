Mechanism axes (A–C)

Built around two open problems rather than around device components:
A. In wide-bandgap absorbers, how much of the loss is processing-induced (morphology, composition, crystallinity) versus intrinsic to the material?
B. In an integrated tandem with many interfaces, which one limits stability, and how would we know?
C. The methods and mechanisms both questions depend on.

Each slice has a reading page (recent years, full abstracts), a complete archive (every match, compact), and where available a reviews-only page. Nothing is discarded — recency governs placement, not inclusion.
